What moves this booking's risk

Bars show each detail's push on the risk, compared with a typical booking (vaccination, 9 days ahead, SMS reminder, no deposit, no plan). Right of the line raises risk, left lowers it. Numbers are percentage points.

Recommended setup: deposits on follow-ups and grooming, plus 40 ranked calls a week. A risk-score deposit rule asks 45% of owners living 15+ km away, against 4–7% of those within 2 km.

Model: logistic regression trained on Jan 2024 to Mar 2025 and tested on Apr to Sep 2025 (ROC AUC 0.70). The call and deposit effects are assumptions, not measurements. The deposit effect comes from owners who chose to pay, so the cautious setting is the safer planning figure. Revenue uses list prices and annualises the 26-week replay.

Northgate, its people and its data are fictional, created for portfolio practice.
